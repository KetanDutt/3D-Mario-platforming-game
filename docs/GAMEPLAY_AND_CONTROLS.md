# Gameplay Mechanics & Controls Guide

This guide covers all player maneuvers, abilities, power-up systems, enemy combat, and scoring mechanics in the **3D Mario Platforming Game**.

---

## 1. Complete Controls Scheme

| Action | Keyboard & Mouse | Gamepad | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>Arrow Keys</kbd> | Left Analog Stick | Smooth 360° directional movement relative to camera |
| **Jump** | <kbd>Space</kbd> | Button A (South) | Jump with coyote time and input buffering |
| **Double Jump** | <kbd>Space</kbd> (after landing Jump 1) | Button A | Slightly higher jump with mid-air pose |
| **Triple Jump** | <kbd>Space</kbd> (after landing Jump 2) | Button A | Highest acrobatic jump with front flip |
| **Wall Slide & Jump** | Hold towards wall in air + <kbd>Space</kbd> | Towards wall + Button A | Slide down wall with spark friction, kick off opposite direction |
| **Crouch** | <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> | Right Trigger / Button B | Shrinks hitbox and lowers center of gravity |
| **Crouch Slide** | Running + <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> | Running + Right Trigger | Slide under low obstacles while preserving forward momentum |
| **Ground Pound** | <kbd>V</kbd> (in mid-air) | Button X / Button B | Flip in air, dive vertically downward, break heavy blocks |
| **Fireball Attack** | <kbd>Left Mouse Click</kbd> | Right Bumper | Shoot ricocheting fireballs when Fire Mario is active |
| **Grab / Throw Shell** | <kbd>Right Mouse Click</kbd> | Left Bumper / Button Y | Hold Koopa Shell; release to kick/throw across ground |
| **Enter Warp Pipe** | <kbd>V</kbd> while standing on Pipe | Down / Button A | Descend into pipe and travel between surface & underground |
| **Orbit Camera** | <kbd>Mouse Drag</kbd> | Right Analog Stick | Orbit camera around Mario |
| **Pause Menu** | <kbd>ESC</kbd> or <kbd>P</kbd> | Start / Menu Button | Open pause overlay with settings, volume & controls |

## 2. Power-up Forms

### 🍄 Regular Mario
- Default form.
- Can run, jump, triple jump, wall jump, crouch, and ground pound.
- Can take 1 hit before losing a life.

### 🔥 Fire Mario
- Obtained by collecting a **Fire Flower** from Question Blocks.
- Cap and shirt turn bright white with red overalls.
- Press **Left Mouse Click** to cast rolling fireballs that bounce along slopes and incinerate enemies.
- Taking damage downgrades Mario back to Regular Mario with a brief invulnerability window.

### ⭐ Mega Mario
- Obtained by collecting the rare **Mega Mushroom**.
- Mario grows to 2.5x normal size accompanied by ground-trembling camera shake.
- Complete invincibility for 18 seconds.
- Destroys brick blocks, crates, and sends enemies flying on contact!

## 3. Enemies & Interactions

### Goomba
- **Behavior**: Patrols platforms. When Mario enters proximity, Goomba reacts with a surprise alert and charges!
- **Counter**: Jump on top to stomp flat into a golden coin (+200 pts) or burn with fireballs.
- **Danger**: Touching from the sides deals damage to Mario.

### Mega Goomba (Boss)
- **Behavior**: Giant Goomba with high endurance (5 HP).
- **Counter**: Requires 5 stomps or multiple fireball barrages to defeat.
- **Reward**: Drops the legendary Mega Mushroom upon defeat!

### Koopa Troopa & Koopa Shells
- **Behavior**: Patrols edges safely.
- **Counter**: Stomp once to force into shell.
- **Shell Mechanics**:
  - Run into a stationary shell to kick it forward.
  - Right-click to pick up and carry the shell; right-click again to throw.
  - Sliding shells bounce off walls, smash brick blocks, and plow through lines of Goombas!

---

## 4. Blocks & Collectibles

- **Question Mark (?) Blocks**: Hit from underneath or ground pound from above to yield coins, Fire Flowers, or mushrooms. Turns into empty bronze block once depleted.
- **Brick Blocks**: Breakable blocks. Smashing with a ground pound or spinning Koopa shell shatters them into debris chunks.
- **Gold Coins**: Collect 100 coins to earn an extra 1-UP life (+100 pts per coin).
- **Green Stars**: Hidden collectibles scattered across courses. Collect all stars to complete stage challenges!
- **Goal Flagpole**: Located at the end of each stage. Jump as high as possible onto the pole to earn maximum bonus points, watch the course clear celebration banner, and complete the level!
